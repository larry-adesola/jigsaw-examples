# Trip budget

**How do I put a small app online that only I can open?**

A budget, a list of what you have spent, and what is left. Until you add someone, you are the only person who can open it.

## Publish it

1. Connect Jigsaw to your AI agent: [jigsawapps.com/agents](https://jigsawapps.com/agents)
2. Open this folder and say "publish this to Jigsaw".
3. On the app's sharing page, add nobody to keep it to yourself, or add the people you are travelling with.

## What it shows

- An app that is private from the moment it is published. There is no public link to switch off.
- Two lists in one app: `budget` holds one record, `expenses` holds one per expense.
- Adding the first record and replacing it afterwards: the budget is a `POST` the first time and a `PUT` after that.

An example app for [Jigsaw](https://jigsawapps.com). More in the [examples repository](https://github.com/larry-adesola/jigsaw-examples).
